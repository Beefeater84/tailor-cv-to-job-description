# CV: education

Degrees, qualifications and professional certifications.

### EDU-01 — What goes in: the highest qualification and relevant certifications
- Applies to: CV / education
- Rule: give the highest qualification (degree or its equivalent) and the professional certifications relevant to the vacancy or the job family. Leave out short courses that add nothing for this role.
- Why: the recruiter checks that the required qualification is there. Every course ever taken buries it, and an old minor course says nothing about the candidate.
- Sources: 0T8q3B82PRw-en.md (section "Addressing Job Gaps and Education", 07:19)
- Agreement: 1 of 4 authors, no conflicts
- Weight: 0.5
- Needs: profile block with education and certifications; requirements of the vacancy or the job family (required degree, certifications)
- Check: the highest qualification is present; every certification listed is relevant to the vacancy or the job family
- Limits: only qualifications from the profile

### EDU-02 — How it is written: brief
- Applies to: CV / education
- Rule: one line per entry: qualification, institution, year. No descriptions of courses or grades unless the vacancy asks for them.
- Why: education is checked, not read. A short section leaves room for experience, which is what gets the interview.
- Sources: 0T8q3B82PRw-en.md (section "Addressing Job Gaps and Education", 07:19)
- Agreement: 1 of 4 authors, no conflicts
- Weight: 0.4
- Needs: profile block with education
- Check: one line per entry
- Limits: none
