# CV: skills

A short list of the candidate's skills: tools, software, methodologies, languages.

### SKILL-01 — Up to ten skills, after the summary and before experience
- Applies to: CV / skills
- Rule: a skills section right under the summary, before the experience (see the section order, GEN-06). At most ten bullets. Skills the vacancy or the job family asks for go first, worded as the vacancy words them.
- Why: recruiters search CVs by keyword, in job boards and internal systems. A short list near the top puts the keywords where both the search and the recruiter's eye find them.
- Sources: 0T8q3B82PRw-en.md (section "Highlighting Skills and Experience", 05:55); position before experience: also owner; vacancy wording first: our addition
- Agreement: 1 of 5 sources, no conflicts
- Weight: 0.8
- Needs: CV block with skills; skills used in the experience; requirements of the vacancy or the job family
- Check: at most ten bullets; the section comes after the summary and before experience; every skill is in the profile
- Limits: only real skills from the profile that the candidate would talk about in an interview. No buzzwords. A skill the vacancy asks for and the profile lacks is not added; the agent asks the candidate (GEN-03).
