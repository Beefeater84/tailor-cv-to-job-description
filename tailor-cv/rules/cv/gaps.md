# CV: career gaps

Periods between jobs: redundancy, burnout, caring for someone, travel, study, visa issues.

### GAP-01 — A gap is filled with a project, not explained by default
- Applies to: CV / experience timeline, and the agent's dialogue with the candidate
- Rule: by default the CV does not explain a gap between jobs. The agent finds the gaps from the dates of the jobs and asks the candidate only whether there was a project in that period (a pet project, open source, study, freelance). Such a project goes into the CV by PROJ-01 with its dates and fills the period. The agent does not ask the reason for the gap. When the candidate wants the reason shown, it is one line next to the project or instead of it, for example "Parental leave" next to a project of that period (GEN-10: the candidate decides).
- Why: people do not like to show a gap and its reason, and a reason the candidate did not want to give helps no one. A project with dates shows that the candidate kept working on their skills, and it is a fact the recruiter can ask about; the source says an unexplained gap makes the recruiter guess, and the project answers that without a story.
- Sources: 0T8q3B82PRw-en.md (section "Addressing Job Gaps and Education", 07:19): give every gap one line with its reason; no explanation by default, the question about a project, the reason only on the candidate's wish: owner
- Agreement: 1 of 4 authors on the subject; owner decided against it: the source explains every gap with its reason, the rule explains none by default and fills the period with a project
- Weight: 0.5
- Needs: dates of the jobs (`../profile-data.md` P-06), to find the gaps; projects outside jobs with their dates and the optional context note the candidate gave (`../profile-data.md` P-07)
- Check: for every gap longer than the threshold, the candidate was asked whether there was a project; every project or reason shown for a gap is in the profile; no reason the candidate did not give
- Limits: the agent never invents a project or a reason to fill a gap, and never asks the reason. A gap with no project and no note stays as it is.

## Open questions

- **What counts as a gap**: the length from which the agent asks about a period between jobs (for example, more than three months).
