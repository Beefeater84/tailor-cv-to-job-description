# CV: career gaps

Periods between jobs: redundancy, burnout, caring for someone, travel, study, visa issues.

### GAP-01 — Explain a gap in one line, never hide it
- Applies to: CV / experience timeline
- Rule: every gap between jobs gets one line in its place in the timeline with the reason, for example "Career break due to redundancy, actively seeking new opportunities." No longer explanation.
- Why: gaps are normal, but an unexplained gap makes the recruiter guess, and guessing goes against the candidate. One line closes the question.
- Sources: 0T8q3B82PRw-en.md (section "Addressing Job Gaps and Education", 07:19)
- Agreement: 1 of 4 authors, no conflicts
- Weight: 0.5
- Needs: dates of all jobs (to find gaps); the reason for each gap from the profile
- Check: every gap longer than the threshold has a line with a reason
- Limits: the reason comes from the profile or from the candidate. When it is missing, the agent asks the candidate (GEN-03) and never makes one up.

## Open questions

- **What counts as a gap**: the length from which a period between jobs needs a line (for example, more than three months).
