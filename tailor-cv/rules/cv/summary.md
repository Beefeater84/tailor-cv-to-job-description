# CV: summary

The short paragraph under the header that says who the candidate is and what role they want.

### SUM-01 — Four lines: who, what, achieved, looking for
- Applies to: CV / summary
- Rule: at most four lines that answer, in this order: who the candidate is (role and total years of experience), what they do, what they have achieved, what role they are looking for. The role is described by its type and the type of company, never by a company name.
- Why: the recruiter reads the summary right after the header. A long paragraph without facts is skipped, and the CV loses the place where it says why the candidate fits.
- Sources: 0T8q3B82PRw-en.md (section "Crafting a Professional Summary", 02:05); total years and no company name: owner; picking what the candidate does and achieved by the job family: our addition
- Agreement: 1 of 5 sources, no conflicts
- Weight: 0.8
- Needs: dates of the candidate's experience (for total years), main duties and achievements from the profile; vacancy fields `title`, `requirements`, the company description
- Check: at most four lines; the four parts are present; no company name; years, numbers and achievements are in the profile
- Limits: years, numbers and achievements only from the profile. Years of experience are the total, not the years relevant to the job family: the bigger number is used. The no-cliché rule (GEN-01) applies here first of all.
