## Job description

[**Manatal**](https://manatal.com/) is an HRTech software service (B2B SaaS) company headquartered in Bangkok, Thailand. Manatal is one of the fastest-growing start-ups in the region and is backed by Surge and Sequoia Capital.

Manatal has a global presence and is trusted by thousands of businesses in over 135 countries. Our goal is to transform the entire hiring process by making it simple, efficient, and enjoyable for recruiters, hiring managers, and candidates alike. Our mission is to offer the best-in-class AI-powered technologies to empower small, medium, and large businesses in their staffing & recruitment transformation.

### **Role Overview**

We are looking for a **Senior Platform Engineer** to join our team. You will focus on improving the reliability, scalability, and performance of our systems, working closely with engineering and operations to ensure smooth, stable, and efficient platform operations. You will also help improve the developer experience by automating workflows and building custom tools.

### **Responsibilities**

- Monitor and operate production systems to ensure high availability and reliability
- Improve observability through better metrics, logging, tracing, and alerting
- Automate manual and repetitive operations tasks to improve efficiency
- Collaborate with engineering teams to design resilient, scalable systems
- Improve developer experience by building custom tools and automating infrastructure
- Participate in on-call rotations and handle incident response
- Conduct post-incident reviews and drive system improvements
- Benchmark, test, and optimize system performance and scalability
- Enhance CI/CD pipelines, deployment workflows, and infrastructure processes

### **Requirements**

- Strong experience with AWS (or other major cloud providers such as GCP or Azure)
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and container orchestration
- Solid Linux systems knowledge
- Programming skills in Golang, Python, Bash, or similar
- Familiarity with CI/CD and automation tools: ArgoCD, Crossplane, GitHub Actions, Terraform, Ansible
- Strong understanding of cloud security best practices and tools such as Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, or HashiCorp Consul
- Experience with observability and monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, Loki, Datadog)
- Solid understanding of networking, load balancing, DNS, and security

### **Nice to Have**

- Background in software engineering
- Strong focus on improving developer experience and setting best practices
- Cross-team collaboration and communication skills
